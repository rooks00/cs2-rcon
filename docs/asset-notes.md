# Generated artwork

The built-in image generation tool produced the original CS2-inspired raster illustration used in `public/images/desert-diorama.webp`. The source was transcoded to WebP without changing its composition. This is a 3D-rendered illustration, not an interactive 3D model or an accurate playable-map preview.

## Asset prompt

Create a premium 3D product illustration asset for a Counter-Strike 2 server management application. Landscape 3:2 image. A beautifully crafted isolated isometric miniature of a Middle Eastern tactical shooter map: a square elevated slab holding weathered warm sandstone buildings and passageways, a monumental arch, stairs, flat roofs, one tall palm tree, stacked wooden crates, subtle pipes and air conditioners, terracotta details, a floor area with a spray-painted orange A bombsite marking. Empty environment, no people, no weapons, no UI, no labels besides the small painted A in the scene. Art directed realistic materials, detailed architectural scale model, warm sunset rim light from upper right, cool soft studio fill. Camera high three-quarter isometric, entire square diorama fully visible with broad margin around it. Background solid near-black graphite #151719 blending into soft grounding shadow, no stars or background landscape. Rich physically rendered octane/Blender quality, sophisticated muted sand palette with occasional orange edge highlights, extraordinary tiny modeled details. High resolution standalone asset with clean silhouette, no text overlays, no watermarks.
